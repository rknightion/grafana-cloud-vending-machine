Mid level · Capability map

What can the machine vend?

A stack is the starting point. The useful service includes access, content, collection and operations, selected according to workload needs and platform policy.

These are resource families in this reference, not a promise that every stack receives every feature. Workload targets, credentials, people, product availability and entitlement remain prerequisites.
