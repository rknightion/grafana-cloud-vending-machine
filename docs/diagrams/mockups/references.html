Geographic land outline

The telecom market illustration embeds simplified Natural Earth land geometry. The markers are approximate business-market anchors, not offices, infrastructure or hosting regions.

  • Natural Earth 1:110m land dataset
  • GeoJSON source used for the inline vector outline
  • Natural Earth public-domain terms of use

The exported diagram contains the land outline, so it does not fetch maps or send market data to a mapping service.
